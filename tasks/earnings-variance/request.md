# Request: Earnings variance memo

**From:** Head of Equity Research
**To:** Equity Research Analyst

**The Procter & Gamble Company** (PG) reported fiscal Q1 FY2027 results (quarter ended 30 September 2026). The earnings release is in `anchor/` and the
consensus estimates are in `companions/consensus.xlsx` (a synthetic sheet prepared for this exercise).

Write a **one-page memo** (`.docx` or `.pdf`) for the investment committee that:
1. States the headline result: revenue, EPS, and any segment or guidance items, versus consensus (beat/miss, in $ and %).
2. Explains the main drivers of each variance, citing figures from the release.
3. Flags anything in the release that affects the quality of the beat or miss (one-offs, tax, buybacks, FX).
4. Summarises guidance versus consensus.
5. Ends with a recommendation: does this change our view, and what should we watch next quarter?

Include a compact variance table. Do not exceed one page.
